## Authoring and release gate

Create original speaking/listening activities, pronunciation drills, reading passages, vocabulary tasks, grammar explanations, writing models and assessments. Use original examples and appropriately licensed texts. A qualified English teacher must check pronunciation notation, grammar, reading-level suitability, cultural relevance and alignment before approval. Do not infer term placement from the order of the contents list.
