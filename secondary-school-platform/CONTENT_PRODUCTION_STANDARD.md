# Learnwise content production standard

## Mandatory content gate
Every lesson must reference an approved curriculum version and source record. The initial library covers English Language, General Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Physics; it is not the complete national compulsory subject package.

## Required lesson package
- Class, subject, verified topic title, learning outcomes and exact source section.
- Teacher-reviewed script, worked examples and formative questions.
- Transcript, glossary, pronunciation notes and accessibility text.
- Audio master, delivery encode, duration, codec, loudness and checksum.
- Rights/permission record for every text, image, voice, music and recording.
- Named author, subject reviewer, audio QA reviewer and approval timestamps.
- Separate answer key, never included in student-facing lesson assets.
- Version number, revision reason and replacement/withdrawal link.

## Two-presenter audio format
1. Presenter A introduces the learning goal and prerequisites.
2. Presenter B asks a realistic learner question.
3. Explain in short sections with pauses and recaps.
4. Narrate worked examples step by step; pronounce symbols clearly.
5. Add a retrieval-practice pause and explain the answer.
6. Close with takeaways and a practice prompt.

## Quality checklist
- Nigerian English pronunciation checked by a human educator.
- No invented curriculum claims, quotations or exam predictions.
- No copyrighted textbook passages or third-party recordings without permission.
- No student names, identifiable voices or personal data in production assets.
- Transcript matches final audio; no clipping, missing sections or incorrect formula readings.
- Immutable versioned masters and checksums; content reviewer signs off before publication.
- Corrections create a new version and preserve the previous audit trail.

No audio is complete until the source-backed script, licensed recording and independent QA record exist.
