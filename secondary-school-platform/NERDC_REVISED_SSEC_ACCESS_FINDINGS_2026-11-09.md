# NERDC revised Senior Secondary Education Curriculum — access findings

**Research date:** 9 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Identify the current official curriculum source for Think Tank's SS1–SS3 subject catalogue and document what is, and is not, authorised for use.

## Official revised SSEC source located

NERDC's official website publishes a dedicated **New Revised Senior Secondary Education Curriculum (SSEC)** landing page with separate links for SS1, SS2 and SS3:

- [NERDC revised SSEC landing page](https://nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/new_senior_curriculum_home.html)
- [SS1 curriculum portal](https://lmis.nerdcportals.com.ng/sss_1)
- [SS2 curriculum portal](https://lmis.nerdcportals.com.ng/sss_2)
- [SS3 curriculum portal](https://lmis.nerdcportals.com.ng/sss_3)

The landing page is an official NERDC source. During this research pass, automated access to the linked curriculum portal returned HTTP 403 for SS1 and SS2 and HTTP 404 for SS3. This does **not** prove that a human with the correct account cannot access them. The actual SS1–SS3 curriculum files/content have therefore **not** been acquired or verified.

## Revised senior-secondary subject structure

NERDC's official subject-offerings document and the Federal Ministry of Education press release dated 3 September 2025 state that the revised curriculum takes effect from the 2025/26 academic year and that senior-secondary students select **8–9 subjects**.

The five core and compulsory subjects are:
1. English Language
2. General Mathematics
3. One trade subject
4. Citizenship and Heritage Studies
5. Digital Technologies

The Science field includes Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Further Mathematics, among other subjects. The Ministry release describes students selecting 8–9 subjects overall; the five requested Think Tank subjects beyond English and General Mathematics are not all universal compulsory subjects. They should be modelled as Science-field choices/electives, subject to the student's actual school pathway and the final official SS1–SS3 curriculum.

Official references:
- [NERDC — Senior Secondary Curriculum Offering (PDF)](https://nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/pdf_files/Basic%20and%20Senior%20Secondary%20Education%20Curriculum%20Offerings.pdf)
- [Federal Ministry of Education — Press Release on New FG Curriculum, 3 September 2025 (PDF)](https://nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/pdf_files/Press%20Release%20On%20New%20FG%20Curriculum.pdf)

## Existing e-Curriculum portal — do not confuse with the revised SSEC source

The NERDC e-Curriculum portal at [nerdc.org.ng](https://www.nerdc.org.ng/Index.aspx) describes access through teacher/student eIDs and a subscription for digital curriculum resources. Its public landing page still describes the 9-year Basic Education Curriculum and its materials are stated to be for personal study. The portal's public subscription page currently displays an annual Senior Secondary (SS1–SS3) price of ₦10,000, but this price and the applicability of that portal to the revised 2025 SSEC must be confirmed directly before any payment.

A portal subscription or ability to view material is **not** permission to copy, republish, adapt, commercialise, or distribute NERDC curriculum content inside Think Tank. No curriculum content has been copied from this portal.

## Impact on Think Tank

- Retain the owner's six requested subjects as the initial product catalogue: English Language, General Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Physics.
- Do not represent all six as compulsory for every student. Support subject/pathway selection and verify each learner's actual subject combination against the applicable school/curriculum rules.
- Add the revised SSEC core subjects missing from the initial six to the curriculum coverage review, but do not silently expand the paid product catalogue or change pricing.
- Do not populate topic sequences, learning outcomes, term placement, assessment objectives or NERDC-verification labels from memory, legacy curricula, search snippets or unauthorised copies.
- No topic records or curriculum source records have been inserted into the managed database as a result of this research.
- Before commercial content production, obtain the actual current SS1, SS2 and SS3 curriculum documents, written reuse/adaptation/distribution permission or a licence, and independent qualified-teacher verification for each selected subject/class combination.
