## Authoring and release gate

For every topic, create original student notes, learning outcomes, diagrams/visuals where useful, worked or practical examples, formative questions, teacher answer key, and an optional two-presenter podcast script. Check all factual claims against the official curriculum and reputable subject references. A qualified Biology teacher must review and approve the material. Do not publish or create a paid entitlement until the source evidence, class mapping, rights review and topic publication requirements are satisfied.
