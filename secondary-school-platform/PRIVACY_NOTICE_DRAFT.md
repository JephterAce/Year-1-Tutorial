## Who this notice is for
Learnwise supports senior-secondary learners. Some learners may be under 18. We provide age-appropriate explanations and will publish a parent/guardian notice and consent flow where legally required. Do not create an account for a child unless the applicable age/guardian requirements have been satisfied.

## Information we may process
- Account details: name, username, email, class, password hash and verification/session records.
- Learning activity: lesson access, progress, saved work and assessment attempts/results.
- Integrity and support records: limited event signals, appeals, question reports and support correspondence.
- Payment metadata: payment reference, amount, currency, status and entitlement; full card credentials are handled by the payment provider, not Learnwise.
- Technical/security data: request logs, device/browser metadata needed for security, and operational diagnostics.
- Optional communications preference, only where separately selected.

We do not need a learner's precise location, government ID, home address or sensitive family details for ordinary learning.

## Why we use information
To create and secure accounts, provide lessons and assessments, calculate results, grant paid access, respond to support requests, protect platform integrity, meet legal/accounting duties and maintain platform security. Optional marketing is separate, off by default and revocable.
